# Interactive workspace design research

Research date: October 15, 2026. Scope: ten real portfolio, studio, and storytelling experiences, followed by an original direction for Zim’s five-domain workspace. This is design research, not an accessibility certification of the reference sites.

## Method and source coverage

Visited the actual experiences in a dedicated native Chrome research window using the computer-use tool. Tested entry, links, menus, collection selection, or scrolling; read the resulting accessibility state and visually inspected rendered states. Primary site text and creator articles were checked with web browsing. Observations below describe actions actually performed. Recommendations are our interpretation.

Discovery included the current [Awwwards 3D collection](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/3d/), [Codrops demos](https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/), and Godly’s indexed [Henry Heffernan listing](https://godly.website/website/749-henry-heffernan). The Godly URL currently redirects to `recent.design/?ref=godly`; its gallery page could not be retrieved by the web reader. Henry’s actual site was inspected independently. The Awwwards Bruno case-study URL timed out, so no unverified award, score, or historical/current-version equivalence is assumed. Gallery recognition is not a substitute for interaction research.

## Original direction: a maker’s open workbench

The page’s job is to help a collaborator or hiring lead understand how Zim connects software, data, neuroscience, medical education, and design. The scene is an authored introduction to that relationship. The real evidence remains readable in the page and project details.

The signature is a single sculpted, open, isometric bench with five original objects in an asymmetric working arrangement. A compact workstation anchors the rear; a notebook and folded study sit near the visitor; a branching specimen and data assembly provide height. Use shared surfaces, contact shadows, and generous clear space to make it feel like one place. Avoid five isolated floating icons or five identical pedestals: those read as navigation decoration rather than a person’s working surface.

“You can tell a lot from someone’s desk.” is a strong opening invitation, provided the next line immediately identifies Zim and his actual work. Suggested supporting line: “I’m Zim Bela — founder, CTO, and hands-on software builder, working across data, neuroscience, and medical education.” The roles come from the confirmed structured record. “Explore my desk” and “View projects” can serve different visitor intentions; résumé and contact remain in the persistent navigation.

Shape should be a restrained conceptual neuron, not an invented scientific reconstruction. | Camera moves slightly closer; an adjacent tracing fragment becomes visible. | Collaborative neuron-tracing research and NeuroStack. The proposed data helix needs care: a literal DNA helix communicates genetics more strongly than analysis. Prefer plotted sheets/bars. If a spiral remains, label it as an abstract data sculpture and avoid implying a genomic project.

## Visual system and composition

Palette: lilac canvas `#e8e7f2`, paper `#f8f8fc`, ink `#252137`, violet `#6648d9`, mint `#9eddc7`, and brass `#dfb65b`. Coral may be confined to the notebook object and should not become a competing global accent. These are art-direction values; final text and focus combinations require measured contrast. Mint and brass work best as material accents with ink text.

Bricolage Grotesque gives the title a human, irregular rhythm; Geist keeps descriptions, controls, dates, and stacks clear. Use the display face sparingly. Keep domain names and selected-state indicators visible in ordinary type. Do not put essential text on a tilted 3D screen.

Desktop: scene occupies most of the opening composition, with introductory copy that is short enough to leave real space for it. Selected content appears in one calm paper panel adjoining the scene. Keep the selected object visible; the camera should reframe its context rather than zoom into an unrecognizable fragment. Subsequent sections use actual product imagery, diagrams, and readable evidence rather than repeating the desk five times.

Mobile: keep an intentional static/low-cost overview and a wrapping row of five domain buttons. A selected story becomes a normal full-width panel below it. Do not shrink desktop labels into tiny targets or force drag/orbit controls. The visual place survives even if motion is reduced.

## Interaction contract

1. First render includes name, real positioning, navigation, and the five domain buttons. The scene can load afterward. No fake wait, boot screen, forced tour, or entry click is required.
2. Pointer hover and keyboard focus identify the same object. Activating either a label or its object selects one domain. Selection updates a visible heading, short story, real project links, and an explicit selected state.
3. Camera motion is short, interruptible, and bounded. Selection during a transition retargets cleanly. “Show whole desk” returns to the overview. Escape closes an expanded story and restores focus to its trigger.
4. “Take a short tour” is optional. Five authored steps connect the domains; Next, Previous, and Exit remain available. Progress means tour position only. Nothing progresses automatically while someone reads.
5. Reduced motion uses the same selection and panel state with a fixed camera and immediate content updates. A flat explorer gives the same stories and destinations in the same order. JavaScript or WebGL failure leaves ordinary readable content and links.
6. Each domain panel explains a connection and points toward evidence. Project details contain the actual role, problem, methods, status, verified links, and source-grounded product workflow. Domain artwork never substitutes for an app demo.

## Critique and acceptance direction

The workbench is a convincing fit because it represents the connective act of making. Its main originality risk is drifting toward the familiar retro-computer room. Keep the open lilac surface, contemporary tools, scientific study objects, custom folded form, and the five-domain narrative. Author meshes, composition, lighting, and motions for this person. The reference sites contribute principles of orientation, direct access, and reversible selection; none supply an asset layout or visual template.

The main execution risks are unreadable miniature content, excessive glass effects, ambiguous object meanings, and an elaborate loading experience. Resolve those in the first playable scene: five labeled selections, a complete flat path, one tested camera move, and a true project link. Add material richness only after that core loop is clear at mobile and desktop sizes.

Implementation review should verify actual touch activation, keyboard selection/focus return, immediate reduced-motion behavior, unavailable/context-lost WebGL, 200% zoom, scene pause offscreen, bounded pixel ratio, and stable resource disposal. Passing those checks belongs in implementation QA; this research does not claim them for the new site.
