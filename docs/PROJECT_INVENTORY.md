- **Caregiver Academy:** `#preview` is a public product preview. The account library is not an unlocked demo. Local lesson/check interactions are adapted educational samples; source attribution and Meili Bela's contribution remain visible.
- **RBT Practice Hub:**19 source tasks,105 original cards and190 original questions are visible in the live app. Practice readiness is a browser-local learning state, not an official assessment or credential. The portfolio's three-task sample is smaller and visit-scoped.
- **StepSpark:** cards `irc-001` and `irc-004` ground the current sample; original/public-domain media and source/review metadata are retained. Concept Map and NBME Challenge are planned prototype panels, not shipped generation capabilities.
- **NeuroStack:** the portfolio sample contains eight units from the first20seconds of the pinned public NWB derivative. DANDI000582 v0.251111.2151 and CC BY4.0 provenance accompany it. Charts are precomputed artifact views; no browser model refit is claimed. Live repair status must be refreshed from the repair owner's final evidence before release.
- **Rethink Automations:** show invented non-clinical queue items and the user-confirmation boundary. The sample changes its own state and does not operate a clinical platform.
- **NeuroPath Insight:** values remain simulated at the point of use. Exploratory model outputs are not validated financial or clinical recommendations.
